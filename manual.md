# APPENDIX H - USER MANUAL
YP4K VILLA BERDE RESORT - BOOKING & TRAIL GUIDE SYSTEM

This user manual provides comprehensive, step-by-step instructions for navigating and utilizing the YP4K Villa Berde Resort Booking & Trail Guide System. It is divided into specific sections for Users (Guests), Staff, and Administrators.

---

## 1. SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
The YP4K Resort System is a responsive, web-based application. No software installation is required. To access and use the system, prepare the following:
Device: Any desktop computer, laptop, tablet, or smartphone (Android or iOS).
Browser: An updated web browser like Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Mozilla Firefox, or Apple Safari.
Internet: A stable internet connection for real-time map navigation, booking availability checks, and payment proof submission.
Email: An active, valid email address for account verification, booking updates, and system alerts.

## 2. ACCESSING THE SYSTEM
Step 1: Open your web browser.
Step 2: Type the YP4K Resort URL into the address bar and press Enter.
Step 3: The Public Home Page will load. From here, you can browse resort information, view the interactive trail guide, or choose to Log In or Register.

---

# PART A – USER (GUEST) GUIDE

## 3. CREATING A USER ACCOUNT (SIGN UP)
Guests who wish to book accommodations, activities, or bundles online must create a user account.
Step 1: On the home page, click Register.
Step 2: Fill in your Personal Information (First Name, Last Name, Phone Number, etc.).
Step 3: Enter a valid Email Address and create a secure password.
Step 4: Click Create Account.
Step 5: An automated email containing a verification code will be sent to your registered email address. Enter this code on the verification screen to activate your account.

## 4. LOGGING IN
Step 1: Click the Login button from the navigation menu.
Step 2: Enter your registered Email Address and Password.
Step 3: Click Log In. The system will authenticate you and direct you to the User Dashboard.

## 5. NAVIGATING THE USER DASHBOARD
After logging in, you will be welcomed to the User Dashboard. The navigation provides quick access to:
   Dashboard: Displays a summary of your active bookings and recent activity.
   Book Now: Launches the online reservation system to browse available rooms, activities, and bundles.
   Trail Guide: Opens the interactive google guide map.
   My Bookings: Allows you to track the approval status of your reservations and view invoices.
   Profile: Update your contact details and account security.

## 6. MAKING A RESERVATION (ROOMS, ACTIVITIES, BUNDLES)
Step 1: Click Book Now from your dashboard.
Step 2: Choose your Booking Category:
Accommodations: (Rooms or Cottages). Select whether you are booking a Day Tour or Overnight stay, and choose your check-in/check-out dates.
Activities: (e.g., Basketball Court, Swimming Pool). Choose your date and time slot.
Bundles: Curated packages combining rooms and activities.
Step 3: Input the number of guests. The system will automatically calculate extra person fees if you exceed the base capacity.
Step 4: Need more rooms? You can click Add Another Room to book multiple rooms under a single reservation.
Step 5: Review the automated price computation and click Proceed to Payment.

## 7. PAYMENT PROCESSING (GCASH QR & PROOF UPLOAD)
The system utilizes a manual GCash QR code verification process.
Step 1: On the Payment Checkout page, a GCash QR code and the official Resort GCash Mobile Number will be displayed.
Step 2: Open your GCash mobile app. Scan the displayed QR code or use "Express Send" to transfer the exact total amount.
Step 3: Save or screenshot the GCash Transaction Receipt on your phone. Take note of the GCash Reference Number.
Step 4: On the system's Payment Confirmation form, click Choose File and upload the screenshot of your GCash receipt (JPG, PNG).
Step 5: Click Submit Reservation. Your booking is now marked as "Pending Review" until the Admin verifies the receipt.

## 8. VIEWING RESERVATION STATUS & INVOICES
Step 1: Navigate to My Bookings.
Step 2: Track the status of your booking (Pending Review, Approved, or Rejected).
Step 3: Once Approved, click on the booking to view your Official Invoice. You can print this invoice or save it on your phone to present at the resort entrance.

## 9. USING THE INTERACTIVE TRAIL GUIDE / MAP
Step 1: Click Trail Guide from the menu.
Step 2: The interactive map displays the entire resort nearby route, highlighting activity areas and trails

## 10. UPDATING YOUR PROFILE
Step 1: Click Profile.
Step 2: You can update your Name, Phone Number, and Profile Picture.
Step 3: You can securely change your password by providing your current password and a new one.

# PART B – STAFF GUIDE

## 11. NAVIGATING THE STAFF DASHBOARD
Staff accounts have a specialized dashboard designed for on-site operations.
Walk-in Booking: Allows staff to quickly process guests who arrive at the resort without a prior online reservation.
Availability Checker: A quick calendar view to see which rooms and activities are occupied or available for the day.

## 12. PROCESSING WALK-IN RESERVATIONS
Step 1: Click Walk-in Booking.
Step 2: Ask the guest for their preferred Accommodation or Activity.
Step 3: The system will immediately check if the requested item is available and respects the Daily Resort Capacity.
Step 4: Fill in the guest's Name and Contact Number.
Step 5: Collect the Cash payment over the counter.
Step 6: Click Confirm Walk-in. The system will bypass the GCash proof requirement and instantly mark the booking as "Approved" and generate a receipt.

## 13. CHECKING AVAILABILITY
Step 1: Go to the Availability tab.
Step 2: Select a date to view all booked slots and occupied accommodations. This prevents staff from double-booking a room that an online user has already reserved.

# PART C – ADMINISTRATOR GUIDE

## 14. NAVIGATING THE ADMIN DASHBOARD
Administrators have full oversight of the resort.
Dashboard: Displays pending bookings, today's expected arrivals, and total registered users.
Bookings Management: Approve or reject online reservations.
Manage Accommodations: Add or edit rooms/cottages.
Manage Activities: Add or edit resort facilities.
Manage Bundles: Create combined promotional packages.
Capacity Control: Set the maximum number of guests allowed inside the resort per day.
Staff Accounts: Create and manage staff logins.
Audit Logs: Track all system actions.

## 15. MANAGING ONLINE BOOKINGS (APPROVE / REJECT)
Step 1: Click Bookings Management.
Step 2: Filter the table by "Pending Review".
Step 3: Click View on a pending booking.
Step 4: The system will display the uploaded GCash Screenshot and Reference Number side-by-side with the booking details.
Step 5: Verify the receipt with your actual GCash merchant account.
To Approve: Click Approve Booking. The guest is notified, and the room is officially locked for them.
To Reject: Click Reject Booking. You must provide a reason (e.g., "Invalid GCash Reference"). The guest is notified, and the room becomes available for others again.

## 16. MANAGING ACCOMMODATIONS (ROOMS / COTTAGES)
Step 1: Navigate to Accommodations.
Step 2: Click Add New Accommodation.
Step 3: Fill out the details: Name, Type (Room/Cottage), Price per Night, Price per Day Tour, Base Capacity, and Extra Person Fee.
Step 4: Set the Daily Booking Limit (e.g., if you have 5 identical standard rooms, set this to 5).
Step 5: Upload an image of the room and click Save.

## 17. MANAGING ACTIVITIES
Step 1: Navigate to Activities.
Step 2: Click Add New Activity.
Step 3: Provide the Name, Description, Price per hour, and Capacity.
Step 4: Upload an image and click Save. You can also toggle an activity "Inactive" if it is under maintenance.

## 18. MANAGING BUNDLE PACKAGES
Step 1: Navigate to Bundles.
Step 2: Click Create Bundle.
Step 3: Name the bundle, set the fixed Price, and specify the valid Tour Type (Day Tour, Overnight, or Both).
Step 4: Select which Accommodations and Activities are included in this bundle using the checkboxes.
Step 5: Click Save. The bundle will now appear as a single bookable item on the guest dashboard.

## 19. SYSTEM CAPACITY CONTROL (DAILY RESORT CAPACITY)
To prevent overcrowding, the Admin can cap the total number of guests allowed per day.
Step 1: Navigate to Capacity Control.
Step 2: The system has a Default Daily Capacity (e.g., 200 persons).
Step 3: To override specific busy dates (like holidays), select a Date from the calendar and enter a Custom Capacity (e.g., 300 persons).
Step 4: The system will automatically block further bookings (both online and walk-in) on that date once the total number of guests reaches this limit.

## 20. STAFF ACCOUNT MANAGEMENT
Step 1: Navigate to Staff Accounts.
Step 2: Click Add Staff.
Step 3: Enter the staff member's Name, Email, and assign a password.
Step 4: The staff member can now log in to the Staff Dashboard to handle walk-ins. You can revoke access anytime by disabling their account.

## 21. TRAIL GUIDE / MAP MANAGEMENT
Step 1: Navigate to Map Management.
Step 2: Update the map background image if the resort layout changes.
Step 3: Click on the map to add or move interactive "Pins" (Markers) representing cottages, pools, and trails.
Step 4: Add descriptions and images to these pins so users can see them on their Trail Guide.

## 22. AUDIT LOGS & SYSTEM SETTINGS
Audit Logs: View a chronological record of every action taken in the system. See exactly which Admin approved a booking, or which Staff processed a walk-in, including timestamps and IP addresses.
Mail Settings: Configure the SMTP credentials used by PHPMailer to send out OTPs and booking alerts.

## 23. TROUBLESHOOTING

Troubleshooting for Guests:
| Problem | What to Do |
| :--- | :--- |
| Cannot log in | Verify your email and password spelling. Ensure Caps Lock is off. If your account is unverified, check your email for the OTP. |
| Did not receive verification email | Check your Spam or Junk mail folder. Confirm you entered your email correctly during registration. You can request a new code from the verification page. |
| Forgot Account Password | Click "Forgot Password?" on the login page. Enter your registered email address and follow the password reset link sent to your inbox. |
| GCash payment proof upload fails | Ensure your receipt image is in JPG, JPEG, or PNG format and the file size does not exceed the limit (usually 5MB). |
| Booking form says "Capacity Exceeded" | The resort has reached its maximum daily guest limit for the date you selected, or the specific room is fully booked. Please choose another date. |
| Booking status remains "Pending" | Payments are reviewed by the administration during business hours. Please allow a few hours for review, or contact the resort with your GCash reference number. |
| Map pins not loading | Check your internet connection and refresh the browser. Ensure JavaScript is enabled on your device. |

Troubleshooting for Staff:
| Problem | What to Do |
| :--- | :--- |
| Cannot process walk-in | Verify the guest's requested dates against the Availability Checker. If the resort is full, the system will prevent the walk-in to avoid exceeding capacity limits. |
| Walk-in missing from dashboard | Ensure you selected the correct category (Accommodation vs Activity) when confirming the walk-in. |

Troubleshooting for Administrators:
| Problem | What to Do |
| :--- | :--- |
| "Duplicate Entry" Error when adding extra rooms | This is usually resolved by appending extension codes to extra room references. (Note: Admin has applied the -EXT patch to fix this). |
| "Bad Gateway" or "Network Error" on Save | This occurs if the uploaded image is too massive for the server to process, or if there is a temporary server mismatch. Resize your image and try again. Ensure all fields are filled. |
| Unable to upload new room image | Ensure the PHP upload limit isn't exceeded and that the "uploads" folder has proper write permissions (777) on the hosting server. |
| Trail guide map is not updating | If you uploaded a new background map, clear your browser cache (Ctrl + F5) to force the new image to load instead of the old cached version. |
| Cannot log into Admin Panel | Double check your admin credentials. If you are redirected back to the login page without an error, ensure session variables are working on the hosting server. |
| No OTP emails being sent | Go to Mail Settings and verify your SMTP credentials. Ensure the App Password is valid and that Port 465 (SSL) or Port 587 (TLS) is open on the server. |

## 24. LOGGING OUT
To exit the system securely:
Step 1: Click the Logout button located in the top navigation bar or mobile menu.
Step 2: The system will terminate your active session and redirect you safely to the Public Home Page or Login screen.
Step 3: Close your web browser tab. 
Always log out to safeguard your personal account and booking records.
